## Images

Add Generate Image, Edit Image, and Image History. Generate Image makes up to four images from a prompt with Grok Imagine on your Grok or X subscription, with a choice of aspect ratio and resolution. Edit Image changes up to three images (the Finder selection by default) with a prompt. Copy, paste, Quick Look, save, or edit any result, and find your last 200 images in Image History.
