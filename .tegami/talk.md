## Talk to Grok

Add Talk to Grok, a spoken conversation with Grok on your Grok or X subscription. Speak and pause, and Grok answers out loud while the words of both sides show. Press ⌘. to stop Grok mid-answer, and copy the transcript when you are done. Pick Grok's voice (or a custom voice ID) and give it instructions in the command preferences.
